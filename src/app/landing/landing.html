Our story

Before there was a name, there was care.

It started with painting homes — and the belief that people deserve to live with dignity.

Our founder did not wait for an organisation to start helping. She saw where she could make a difference and offered what she could: her time, her effort and her care.

Tend N Keep grows from that same spirit. We want to bring people together across South Africa to respond to hardship, share opportunities and help one another move forward.

Children hold a special place in this mission because they do not choose the circumstances they are born into. Our care also reaches towards the families and communities around them. When communities grow stronger, more people have room to thrive.
